Advanced Ranch Upgrades

From Basic to Pro-Rancher

To truly master the Far, Far Range, you'll need to invest your Newbucks wisely in powerful upgrades. These enhancements automate your ranch, boost profits, and allow you to explore further and safer than ever before.

Corral Enhancements

The heart of your ranch. Keeping slimes happy and contained is your first priority.

  • Music Box: Calms slimes down, reducing their agitation and making them less likely to try and escape. A must-have for energetic or numerous slimes.
  • Plort Collector: Automatically gathers plorts from the corral floor. A huge time-saver and essential for large-scale ranching.
  • Auto-Feeder: Dispenses food at a regular interval. Prevents slimes from getting hungry and agitated.

Farming and Resources

A self-sufficient ranch is a successful ranch. Automate your food production to keep up with demand.

  • Gardens & Silos: Build gardens to grow fruits and veggies. Silos allow you to store a massive amount of any item, preventing resources from rotting on the ground.
  • Nutrient Soil & Sprinkler: Garden upgrades that make crops grow faster and double their yield, respectively.
  • Coops: For ranchers focusing on meat-eating slimes, coops provide a steady supply of Hen Hens and Roostros.

Personal Gear & Vacpack

Your most important tool. Upgrading it unlocks new abilities and makes every task easier.

  • Jetpack: Grants vertical mobility, allowing you to reach new heights and hidden areas. Essential for exploration.
  • Tank Booster: Increases the carrying capacity of your Vacpack slots, letting you carry more items at once.
  • Heart Module: Increases your maximum health, giving you more survivability in dangerous areas.
